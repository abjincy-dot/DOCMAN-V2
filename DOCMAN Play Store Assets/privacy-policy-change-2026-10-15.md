## Is a change required?

device or shared, and no permission was added. The Play Data safety form stays as it is.
Two statements in Section 6 had become wrong, which is why the text was updated:

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| Alerts show a document name or reminder title on the lock screen ("Passport expires today") | Alert text is generic and never contains a name or title ("A document expires today. Open DOCMAN to see which.") |
| Expiry alert arrives twice on the day, morning and late afternoon | 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before; every 30 min 07:00-21:00 on the day until acknowledged; daily for up to a week after |
