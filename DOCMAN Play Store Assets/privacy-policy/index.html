Privacy Policy

Last updated · September 15, 2026

In short: DOCMAN stores everything on your device. We do not operate any server, and the app does not transmit your documents, notes, or personal information anywhere. Its text-recognition library sends Google only limited technical diagnostics (see Section 8).

This Privacy Policy describes how the DOCMAN Android application (“DOCMAN,” “the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information. DOCMAN is developed and maintained by Abish Rajan, contactable at abishrajan6@gmail.com.

01Information DOCMAN Stores, and Where

All of the following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage area. None of it is uploaded to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party by DOCMAN itself.

  • Documents you import (PDFs, images, Word/Excel files, etc.) — stored in the app's private native storage on your device.
  • Folder and department structure, names, colors, and icons — stored locally.
  • Notes you create — stored locally.
  • Edited copies — when you sign, highlight, fill in, erase or crop a PDF, or edit an image, DOCMAN saves the result as a new file in the same folder on your device and leaves the original unchanged. Erasing redraws the affected pages on your device so the erased content is removed from the copy; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Reminders you create (title, due date, and time) — stored locally.
  • Favorites, recents, and search history — stored locally.
  • Items you delete — held in the app's local Recycle Bin until you permanently delete them or the retention period elapses, then removed from the device.
  • App Lock PIN — stored on-device only as a one-way hash (see Section 5). We never see or store your actual PIN.

Uninstalling the app, or using the in-app “Erase All Data” option, removes this data from your device. See Section 9 for backup files, which are the one copy of your data that leaves the app (into a location you choose).

02Google Drive Import

DOCMAN lets you import a file from Google Drive. This works by handing off to the Google Drive app already installed on your device through Android's standard file-picker system — the same mechanism used for “Choose Files.” DOCMAN does not sign in to your Google account, does not request or receive an OAuth access token, and does not communicate with Google's servers directly. The Google Drive app itself handles your authentication and file access under your existing Google account and Google's own privacy terms; DOCMAN only receives the file you explicitly chose, exactly as if you had picked it from local storage.

If the Google Drive app is not installed, this feature falls back to Android's general file picker, which does not involve Google Drive at all.

03Camera and Document Scanning

DOCMAN's “Take Photo” option launches your device's built-in camera app via Android's standard camera intent. DOCMAN does not access the camera hardware directly — the camera app itself captures the photo and hands the resulting image back to DOCMAN, the same way picking an existing photo from your gallery works.

DOCMAN’s “Scan” button (also under Upload → Scan Document) opens Google’s ML Kit document scanner, which is provided by Google Play services on your device. The scanner uses the camera through Google Play services — DOCMAN itself has no camera permission — and, as Google describes it, operates on your device to find the page edges, crop and straighten each page, apply filters and assemble the pages. The finished PDF is handed back to DOCMAN, which saves it in the folder you have open. Google may send the limited technical diagnostics described in Section 8 about how the scanner is working; your scanned pages are not part of them.

04On-Device Text Recognition (OCR)

For scanned or image-only PDF pages — where the page is really just a picture, with no underlying selectable text — DOCMAN uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition to make that page's text selectable, highlightable, and searchable. This processing happens entirely on your device: no page image and no recognized text is ever sent to Google or to any server. ML Kit currently supports Latin-script text only. See Section 8 for how ML Kit is licensed, and for a note on the internet permission this library requires.

05Biometric / PIN App Lock

If you enable fingerprint or face unlock, this is handled entirely by Android's built-in biometric system (BiometricPrompt). DOCMAN never receives, sees, or stores your fingerprint or facial data — that stays inside your device's secure hardware, managed by Android itself. DOCMAN only receives a yes/no result (“unlocked” or “not unlocked”).

If you set a PIN instead, DOCMAN stores a salted, one-way cryptographic hash of it on your device — never the PIN itself — and this hash is never transmitted anywhere. Repeated incorrect PIN attempts are slowed with an increasing delay to resist guessing; there is no way to recover a forgotten PIN other than the app's own reset flow, since DOCMAN itself never has access to it.

06Notifications and Reminders

DOCMAN can show local notifications for two things you set up yourself: expiry alerts, when a document you've tagged with an expiry date is approaching or overdue, and Reminders, a title, due date, and time you set yourself under the Reminders tab. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device by Android itself. DOCMAN does not use any push-notification or cloud-messaging service, and no data leaves your device to generate or deliver them.

To show these, DOCMAN requests Android's notification permission the first time it needs it, and — separately — may ask you to allow “Alarms & reminders,” so Android delivers a scheduled reminder at its set time while the app isn't open. Declining either of these just means notifications may not fire reliably or at all; DOCMAN's other features are unaffected either way, and nothing about this exchanges any data with us or anyone else — it only changes what Android itself allows the app to do on your device.

07Network Access

DOCMAN does not require or use internet access for its core functionality — importing, organizing, viewing, searching, and managing your documents all work entirely offline. The internet is involved in only two situations, and neither sends your documents anywhere: when you buy or restore DOCMAN Pro, which Google Play handles (see Section 8), and when you open an external link — for example the Play Store listing from “Check for Updates,” or the Google Drive app as described in Section 2 — which hands off to another app rather than DOCMAN making a network request itself.

The app declares Android's internet permission, and alongside it the network-state permission (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), which lets an app check whether a connection exists. No DOCMAN feature uses them to send your documents, notes, or personal information anywhere. They are present because the Google ML Kit library described in Section 4 uses them to send Google limited technical diagnostics about the library itself (see Section 8). Both will therefore appear in the app's permission list on Google Play. Purchases of DOCMAN Pro are carried out by the Google Play Store app on your device rather than by DOCMAN itself — see Section 8.

08Analytics, Advertising, and Third-Party SDKs

DOCMAN does not include any analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not know how many people use the app, what they do inside it, or anything else about app usage, because we never collect that information.

The app is built with the following open-source components, which run entirely on your device and do not transmit data on their own: Capacitor (app framework), an Android PDF viewer library, a PDF processing library (for text extraction, highlighting, and form/annotation features), and AndroidX libraries (including the biometric library).

DOCMAN also includes Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition, see Section 4, and the document scanner, see Section 3) — a Google SDK, not open source, distributed under Google's own APIs Terms of Service. The actual text recognition happens entirely on your device, requires no Google account sign-in, and transmits no document content anywhere. However, like Google's other Android SDKs, ML Kit sends Google limited technical diagnostics about how the library is working: device details (manufacturer, model, and Android version), app details (package name and version), a per-installation identifier, performance measurements, feature settings, and error codes. Google encrypts this in transit and does not transfer it to third parties, as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure. It never includes your documents, page images, or recognized text, and it goes to Google, not to us — DOCMAN has no account that receives it. This is why DOCMAN's app package declares Android's internet permission (see Section 7).

DOCMAN uses the Google Play Billing Library to offer the optional one-time DOCMAN Pro upgrade. When you buy or restore DOCMAN Pro, the purchase is processed by the Google Play Store app on your device and by Google, under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. DOCMAN never receives your payment details. The only thing it learns is whether your Google account owns DOCMAN Pro, and it keeps that yes/no answer on your device so the upgrade keeps working when you are offline. No document, note, or file content is ever part of a purchase.

09Backup and Sharing

DOCMAN's “Backup & Export” feature creates a single backup file containing your documents, notes, and folder structure. Before saving it, you choose either to create your own password or to have DOCMAN generate a random recovery key for you; the backup is then encrypted with AES-256 using that password or key. The file is also saved directly to your device's Documents folder, and you can additionally share it using Android's standard share sheet — to Google Drive, email, another app, or elsewhere, entirely at your choice. DOCMAN does not automatically upload this backup anywhere.

Your password or recovery key is never stored or transmitted by DOCMAN — it exists only in your own memory or wherever you chose to save it. Anyone who obtains the backup file cannot open the documents inside it without that password or recovery key, and DOCMAN has no way to recover a lost one: losing it means that specific backup can no longer be restored.

When you use “Share” on an individual document, DOCMAN provides that one file to the app you choose via Android's standard, restricted file-sharing mechanism (FileProvider), scoped only to the file you're sharing.

10Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete individual files, folders, or notes through the app's normal interface (moved to Recycle Bin, then permanently removable).
  • Use Settings → Erase All Data to remove all documents, notes, and the Recycle Bin from the app (this keeps your folder/department structure, now empty — see the in-app confirmation for exactly what's removed).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device, per standard Android behavior.

“Permanently delete” in DOCMAN removes a file from the app's storage and from the device; it is not a forensic secure-erase and does not guarantee the underlying flash storage blocks are unrecoverable. Because nothing is stored on a server we control, there is no separate “delete my account” process — deleting the data on your device is complete and final from DOCMAN's side.

11Children's Privacy

DOCMAN is a general-purpose document management tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from children, and per Section 1, it does not collect information from anyone in the first place beyond what's stored locally on the device it's installed on.
