DOCMAN - Document Manager

Scan, organize and protect all your documents — fast, private, on your phone.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max) — paste everything between the lines
------------------------------------------------------------------
DOCMAN is a fast, private document manager that keeps everything — PDFs, images, Word and Excel files, and your own notes — organized into departments and folders, entirely on your device.

Organize your way
Sort documents into departments (Personal, Work, Finance, Education, Health, ID & Legal, Home and more) and nested folders. Each department gets its own color and an icon you can change. Mark favorites, and find anything instantly with search and sort.

A real PDF viewer, built in
No more bouncing out to another app. DOCMAN's PDF viewer includes:
• Bookmarks and a table of contents
• Search inside the document, zoomed straight to each match
• Highlights, signatures and fillable forms
• Erase with a brush — text, pen marks and stains stay gone in the saved copy — or black out private details
• Crop pages, night mode, rotate, two-page view and page thumbnails
• Password-protected PDF support
• Select and copy text, print, and export a copy
• On-device text recognition (OCR) for scanned pages, so even photocopies become searchable

Scan paper documents
Scan finds the page edges automatically, captures the page, sharpens it with filters and saves a clean multi-page PDF right in the folder you're in — perfect for bills, forms and certificates.

A full image editor, built in
Crop, rotate, flip, draw, highlight, blur out private details, or add text. Adjust brightness, contrast, exposure, saturation and hue, or apply a filter — Document and Straighten tidy up a photographed page. Undo, redo and reset to the original are always there.

Turn photos into PDFs
Convert any image to a PDF in one tap, or queue several photos and save them as a single multi-page PDF — useful for receipts, ID pages or a form you photographed.

Never miss a deadline
Set an expiry date on any document (passport, license, insurance). Your phone alerts you 7 days before, 3 days before, and again on the day until you acknowledge it — even when the app is closed. Add reminders with a title, date and time for anything else you need to track.

Notes, right alongside your files
Jot down a quick note in any folder — no separate app needed.

Your data stays yours
DOCMAN has no server, and your documents are never uploaded — everything lives in the app's private storage on your device. Lock the whole app with a PIN or your fingerprint or face; with DOCMAN Pro you can also lock individual departments, folders, files and notes. When you want to move data off your phone, you're in control: encrypted, password-protected backups that you export yourself.

Try every Pro tool free, then unlock it once
Scanning, signing, highlighting, form filling, erasing, cropping, reading scanned pages, the image editor, PDF conversion and locking individual items can each be used once for free. Unlock DOCMAN Pro with a single one-time purchase for unlimited use, plus unlimited departments and folders. No subscription.

Also included
• Recycle Bin for anything you delete, so mistakes aren't permanent
• Storage dashboard to see what's taking up space
• Light and dark themes
• Import from Google Drive, your camera or any file picker

DOCMAN is built for anyone who's tired of hunting through a messy Downloads folder or a dozen scattered apps just to find one document. Everything in one place, organized the way you think, and never leaving your phone unless you say so.
