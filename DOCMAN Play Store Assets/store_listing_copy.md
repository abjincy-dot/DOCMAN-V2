## Full description (max 4000 chars)

DOCMAN is a fast, private document manager that keeps everything — PDFs, images, Word/Excel files, and your own notes — organized into departments and folders, entirely on your device.

**Organize your way**
Sort documents into departments (Personal, Work, Finance, Education, Health, ID & Legal, Home, and more) and nested subfolders. Customize each department's color and icon, star your favourites, and find anything instantly with search and sort.

**A real PDF viewer, built in**
No more bouncing out to another app. DOCMAN's native PDF viewer includes:
- Bookmarks and a table of contents
- Full-text search across the document
- Highlight, signature, and fillable forms
- Erase or redact any area — text, marks and stains stay gone in the saved copy; crop pages too
- Night mode, rotate, spread view, and page thumbnails
- Password-protected PDF support
- Copy text, select text, and export a marked-up copy
- On-device text recognition (OCR) for scanned pages, so even photocopies become searchable

**Scan paper documents**
Scan Document finds the page edges automatically, captures it, sharpens it with filters and builds a clean multi-page PDF right in the folder you're in — perfect for bills, forms and certificates.

**A full image editor, built in**
Photograph a document and clean it up without leaving DOCMAN. Crop, rotate, flip, draw, highlight, blur out private details, or add text. Adjust brightness, contrast, exposure, saturation and hue, or apply a filter — Document and Straighten tidy up a photographed page. Undo, redo, and reset to the original are always there.

**Turn photos into PDFs**
Convert any image to a PDF in one tap, or queue several photos and save them as a single multi-page PDF — useful for receipts, ID pages, or a multi-page form you photographed.

**Never miss a deadline**
Set an expiry date on any document (passport, license, insurance) and get notified before it lapses. Add stand-alone Reminders with a title, date, and time for anything else you need to track.

**Notes, right alongside your files**
Jot down a quick note in any folder — no separate app needed.

**Your data stays yours**
DOCMAN doesn't have a server, and your documents are never uploaded — everything lives in the app's private storage on your device. Lock the whole app with a PIN or your fingerprint/face; with DOCMAN Pro you can also lock individual departments, folders, files and notes. When you do want to move data off-device, you're in control: encrypted, password-protected backups you export yourself.

**Try every Pro tool free, then unlock it once**
Document scanning, signing, highlighting, form filling, erasing and redaction, cropping, reading scanned pages, the image editor, PDF conversion and per-item locking can each be used once for free. Unlock DOCMAN Pro with a single one-time purchase for unlimited use, plus unlimited departments and folders. No subscription.

**Also included**
- Recycle Bin for anything you delete, so mistakes aren't permanent
- Storage dashboard to see what's taking up space
- Light and dark themes
- Import from Google Drive, camera, or any file picker

DOCMAN is built for anyone who's tired of hunting through a messy Downloads folder or a dozen scattered apps just to find one document. Everything in one place, organized the way you think about it, and never leaving your phone unless you say so.
